Air Force Cloud One Breached, Switzerland Sets 24-Hour Patch Standard After AI Attacks

DoD platform hacked, Swiss officials confirm AI-driven intrusions on critical infrastructure.

Hackers Breach Air Force Cloud One Platform

Hackers breached the Air Force's Cloud One computing platform, discovered during a system upgrade within the past month. The platform supports systems tracking munitions at over 200 locations and air defense data feeds across North America. U.S. Cyber Command is assessing the damage, but the attackers, scope, and whether data was stolen remain unknown. [1]

Switzerland Urges 24-Hour Patching After AI-Powered Attacks Succeed

Switzerland's National Cyber Security Centre confirmed its first AI agent attacks, with all known cases successfully compromising victims including two critical infrastructure operators. The agency now advises installing critical updates on internet-facing systems within 24 hours of release. Officials say the number of internet-accessible attack agents has nearly doubled since October. [2]

Drupal Warns of Active Exploitation of Critical Access Bypass

Drupal confirmed attackers are actively exploiting CVE-2026-136716, a critical access bypass that lets attackers take over websites without authentication. This follows a separate Drupal installer flaw patched November 18 that was exploited within roughly a day. The November 25 advisory covers sites left in incomplete install states despite earlier fixes. [3]

VMware vCenter Zero-Day Exploited Since September, CISA Deadline Passed

Broadcom patched CVE-2026-123890, a critical authentication bypass in VMware vCenter, on November 19 after China-linked group Armory Castle exploited it as a zero-day since early September. The flaw carries a CVSS score of 9.8 and allowed unauthenticated attackers to gain administrator access. CISA added it to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog with a November 23 federal deadline. [4]
