CISA flags three exploited flaws; Pentagon cyber strategy still pending

MikroTik and ScreenConnect bugs confirmed exploited; DOD strategy delayed past early September target

MikroTik RouterOS chain exploited before patches shipped

CISA added two MikroTik RouterOS vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on September 10. CVE-2026-67276 and CVE-2026-86060, dubbed MikroTrick by CERT Polska, chain together to grant unauthenticated remote attackers full administrative control of any RouterOS device with SSH exposed to the internet. Exploitation began September 2, one day before MikroTik released patches. Over 122,000 RouterOS SSH instances were internet-facing as of September 5. [1]

ConnectWise ScreenConnect flaw exploited in worm-like attacks

CISA added CVE-2026-84869 to its KEV catalog on September 11 after confirming active exploitation since at least August 20. The critical flaw in ScreenConnect remote access software allows attackers to transfer and execute files through active remote sessions without authorization. Huntress reported incidents where modified ScreenConnect instances deployed VBScript payloads that established persistence and propagated to other clients. Federal agencies had until September 14 to patch. [2]

Pentagon cyber strategy emphasizes offensive operations, release timing unclear

The Defense Department's new cyber strategy, initially expected in early September, will focus on delivering high-impact offensive cyber options to the President and Defense Secretary. Deputy principal cyber advisor Brig. Gen. William Wilburn said the strategy aims to move away from persistent engagement toward options that are bigger and more muscular. The strategy was under development by assistant defense secretary Katie Sutton's office and will be the first overarching DOD cyber framework since 2023. [3]
