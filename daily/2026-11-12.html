Starshield throttled, AI finds 948 kernel flaws, CISA flags six exploited bugs

Ukraine reports military satellite restrictions, Microsoft patches 1,200+ flaws, Maersk breach confirmed.

Ukraine says Starshield speed-limited again, hampering drone defense

<cite index="47-1,47-9,47-10">President Zelensky said Thursday that Starshield has been slowed to 100 mph across Ukraine's front line for several days, with systematic speed restrictions affecting operations.</cite> <cite index="51-4">The restrictions prevent Ukrainian interceptors from reaching sufficient speed to down Russian jet-powered drones.</cite> <cite index="47-4,47-6">The service was similarly slowed in mid-October; Musk initially denied responsibility but later said Ukraine violated terms and conditions, though the U.S. government controls Starshield.</cite> <cite index="48-7">Ukraine's Starshield contract with the U.S. expires next year.</cite> [1]

OpenAI cyber model exploits 948 Linux kernel flaws at $1,279 per bug

<cite index="67-1,67-3,67-5">OpenAI disclosed November 4 that an autonomous pipeline using its unreleased cyber model found 2,308 candidate flaws, validated 1,724, and built working root exploits for 948 distinct Linux kernel vulnerabilities at an API cost of $1.2 million.</cite> <cite index="67-8">The scan ran on the latest kernel in mid-October 2026.</cite> <cite index="68-8,68-9">OpenAI has submitted hundreds of reports and patches for critical flaws; within 10 days during the scan, over 100 flaws were patched and 300 more publicly discussed.</cite> [2]

CISA flags six actively exploited flaws including Cisco BroadWorks telecom bug

<cite index="57-1,57-2">CISA added six vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog November 10, including CVE-2026-20320 in Cisco BroadWorks, CVE-2026-124194 in Microsoft Outlook, and CVE-2026-125350 in Google Chrome.</cite> <cite index="55-3">Federal agencies must patch CVE-2026-20320 by November 24 under BOD 26-04.</cite> <cite index="55-4,55-5">Threat intelligence firm Malbear Labs reported a Chinese-speaking actor exploited the BroadWorks XXE flaw using a jailbroken DeepSeek AI model against a European telecom provider and thirteen organizations.</cite> [3]

Microsoft patches over 1,200 flaws including actively exploited Outlook zero-day

<cite index="24-1">Microsoft released November 2026 Patch Tuesday updates on November 10, addressing 1,235 vulnerabilities.</cite> <cite index="21-1,21-3,21-4">The update includes 1,328 vulnerabilities affecting a range of products, with 60 marked critical and 45 of those being remote code execution flaws.</cite> <cite index="57-2">CVE-2026-124194, an Outlook flaw involving missing user interaction for potentially unsafe actions, is actively exploited and now in CISA's KEV catalog.</cite> [4]

Maersk confirms ransomware breach of legacy logistics system

<cite index="42-1,42-3">Maersk confirmed Tuesday that WhiteArmor ransomware group breached its security via a legacy platform from the 2022 LF Logistics acquisition.</cite> <cite index="42-4,42-6">The group threatens to release 100GB of data including over 29,000 driver records and 16,000 staff accounts, demanding $5 million in bitcoin within 30 days.</cite> <cite index="42-7,42-8">Maersk claims no sensitive data disclosed and the incident is isolated to a local system with no impact on other Maersk systems or customer integrations.</cite> [5]
