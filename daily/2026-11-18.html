Java4Shell RCE, Puppet exploitation, and supply chain breaches dominate

Critical pre-auth flaws under active exploit, plus Uber driver data exposed via AI vendor

Java4Shell disclosed: pre-authentication RCE in OpenJDK 17+

Oracle patched CVE-2026-114938 on November 17, a memory corruption flaw in OpenJDK's Base64 decoder affecting Java 17, 21, 25, and 27. Researchers demonstrated unauthenticated remote code execution against Jira, Jenkins, and Keycloak. Oracle rates it CVSS 8.1; discoverers rate it 9.2 critical. No confirmed exploitation yet, but proof-of-concept code is public. [1]

CISA adds exploited Puppet RCE flaw to KEV catalog

CISA added CVE-2026-115731 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on November 17 after confirmed exploitation. The critical deserialization flaw in Puppet Enterprise and Puppet Core allows remote code execution when attackers hold a valid certificate. Perforce patched on November 3; federal agencies must remediate by December 1. [2]

Uber notifies 149,000 drivers after Fireworks AI breach

Uber disclosed that attackers used compromised Fireworks AI credentials to access driver's license images, names, addresses, and dates of birth for nearly 150,000 drivers. The breach occurred in October; Uber confirmed data access on October 13. Notion and Cursor were also affected by the same Fireworks AI incident. [3]

ConnectWise ScreenConnect partners breached via third-party extensions

ConnectWise disclosed unauthorized access to ScreenConnect partner accounts on November 11, linking the activity to five third-party extensions. Attackers deployed rogue remote-access clients to at least 288 endpoints across 48 organizations. ConnectWise disabled the extensions in 400 cloud partner environments; on-premises users must act manually. [4]
