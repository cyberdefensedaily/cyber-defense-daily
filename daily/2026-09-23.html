Critical ISE Flaw Exploited, Japan Breach Confirmed, DPRK Campaign Attributed

Active exploitation, government intrusions, and North Korean crypto theft dominate the week

Cisco confirms active exploitation of ISE authentication bypass

Cisco disclosed CVE-2026-76460, a CVSS 10.0 authentication bypass in Identity Services Engine, with confirmed exploitation in the wild. The flaw allows unauthenticated remote attackers to gain root-level access through an insufficiently protected API endpoint. CISA added it to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on September 16 with a three-day federal remediation deadline. No workarounds exist; patching is the only remediation. [1]

Japan's Digital Agency discloses breach of 246,000 government records

Japan's Digital Agency confirmed attackers exploited a VPN appliance vulnerability to access its Government Solution Service, exposing approximately 246,000 records of government officials and contractors. The agency detected suspicious activity on June 25 and traced the intrusion to a medium-severity VPN flaw by July 9. Exposed data includes names, email addresses, phone numbers, and workplace addresses; financial information was not affected. [2]

Four nations attribute WaterPlum campaign to North Korea's 313 General Bureau

Japan, the U.S., Australia, and Germany jointly attributed the WaterPlum campaign to North Korea's Munitions Industry Department in a September 18 advisory. The operation compromised at least 30,000 devices across 100+ countries between December 2025 and July 2026, stealing $10.71 million from over 7,000 cryptocurrency wallets. Attackers posed as recruiters for AI and crypto firms, delivering malware through fake coding assessments hosted on GitHub and npm. [3]

Oracle patches 800+ vulnerabilities in September security update

Oracle released its September 2026 Critical Patch Update addressing more than 800 vulnerabilities across 17 product families. More than 100 flaws are rated critical, while over 240 can be exploited remotely without authentication. Affected products include E-Business Suite, Fusion Middleware, Hyperion, Siebel CRM, Analytics, Communications, and Virtualization. [4]
