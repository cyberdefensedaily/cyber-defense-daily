FBI Database Breach, NSA Outage, and Payment Processor Hack Mark Turbulent Week

Critical infrastructure and law enforcement systems face mounting security incidents across government and private sectors

FBI Investigating Apparent Breach of National Crime Database

The FBI is investigating an apparent hack of its National Crime Information Center (NCIC), the nationwide database used by federal, state, and local law enforcement to share warrants, missing persons reports, and criminal records. The breach was first reported November 20 and confirmed by law enforcement sources to multiple outlets, though the FBI has declined to comment publicly. The scope of the intrusion and what data may have been accessed remains unclear, and there is no indication yet whether it is connected to a September breach of the FBI's job portal by the ShinyHunters hacking group. [1]

NSA Network Down for Days After PHP Vulnerability Patching

The National Security Agency's classified internal communications network, NSANet, experienced extended downtime this week after the agency applied urgent patches to address critical PHP vulnerabilities discovered last month. Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton confirmed the outage was linked to the patching effort, not a cyberattack. The NSA restricted campus access to essential personnel only on Tuesday night as it worked to restore the network, though the agency stated mission-critical capabilities remained operational throughout. [2]

Bell Canada Discloses Cyberattack Exposing Customer Data

Bell Canada confirmed that an unauthorized party accessed an internal system on November 17, compromising personal information of residential and business customers across Bell, Bell Aliant, Bell MTS, Virgin Plus, and Lucky Mobile. The company described the threat actor as 'agentic' and said it immediately contained the incident and removed the attacker, with no impact to customer services. Bell has not disclosed the number of affected customers or the specific types of data accessed. [3]

Cleverbridge Payment Processor Breach Exposes Card Data

Payment and subscription platform Cleverbridge confirmed attackers copied payment card data stored on its systems, including card numbers, expiry dates, and CVV codes. The company detected the unauthorized activity on November 4 and began notifying affected customers on November 20. Cleverbridge, which processes checkouts and subscriptions for other companies, has not disclosed how many customers were impacted or which merchant clients may be affected by the breach. [4]

Critical NGINX Vulnerability Allows Remote Code Execution

Rapid7 disclosed CVE-2026-123007, a critical integer underflow vulnerability in NGINX HTTP/2 that can lead to unauthenticated remote code execution under certain conditions. The vulnerability, with a CVSS score of 9.2, was disclosed to F5 on November 12 and publicly announced November 18 following coordinated disclosure. F5 has released patches, and the vulnerability affects the widely-deployed web server software that powers a significant portion of internet infrastructure. [5]
