AI Agents Used in Live Attacks, Critical Flaws Patched, Japan Cloud Ransomware Disrupts 495 Organizations

China-based attacker deploys AI in Korea bank breach; critical NetScaler, Splunk flaws disclosed.

China-Based Attacker Used AI Agent to Breach South Korean Banks

<cite index="57-4,57-6">CrowdStrike reported that a Chinese-speaking hacker used the ARTEX AI penetration testing suite and Claude agents to attack multiple South Korean banks earlier this month, including Shinhan Bank, KB Kookmin Bank, and Hana Bank, exposing customer personal data and credit card information.</cite> <cite index="61-2">The campaign, active from late September to early October 2026, exposed personal information belonging to 68,000 people, according to investigators in Seoul.</cite> <cite index="61-1,61-6">CrowdStrike said the attacker used ARTEX alongside multiple AI models, with the observed instance using DeepSeek v4.1-flash as its primary backend, supplemented by GLM-5.3 and Grok 4.6.</cite> <cite index="65-7,65-8">Following CrowdStrike's public attribution linking ARTEX to the breaches, the tool's developer made it closed-source and discontinued further updates.</cite> [1]

Ransomware Attack on SoftBank Cloud Unit Disrupts 495 Japanese Organizations

<cite index="66-13">IDC Frontier Cloud, owned by SoftBank, said 495 companies and local government services were affected by a ransomware attack, creating a supply chain crisis across Japan.</cite> <cite index="67-3,67-4">The attack on IDCF Cloud's East Japan Region 1 occurred on October 7, affecting almost 500 corporate and municipal customers, with virtual servers in four availability zones stopped and unable to restart.</cite> <cite index="67-5">The company warned it would be difficult to retrieve or restore customer data from affected infrastructure, with customers potentially having to restore everything from their own backups.</cite> <cite index="69-5,69-6">JR East said about 6.09 million customer accounts may have been compromised, while JR Kyushu said about 1.3 million customer accounts may have been compromised as a result of the attack.</cite> [2]

Citrix Ships Third Emergency NetScaler Patch in Two Weeks

<cite index="49-1,49-3,49-4">Citrix urged customers to patch another critical NetScaler vulnerability after weeks of disclosures involving actively exploited flaws. CVE-2026-107406 affects NetScaler ADC and NetScaler Gateway and can lead to remote code execution or denial of service, carrying a CVSS v4.0 score of 9.5.</cite> <cite index="52-2,52-9">The memory overflow vulnerability affects Citrix NetScaler ADC and NetScaler Gateway under specific SAML authentication configurations, associated with deployments configured as a SAML Service Provider or SAML Identity Provider.</cite> <cite index="52-4">Citrix stated it was not aware of any unmitigated exploitation at the time of publishing its advisory.</cite> <cite index="53-8,53-9">Shadowserver is tracking over 21,000 Internet-exposed IP addresses with NetScaler fingerprints, nearly 20,000 ADC appliances and just over 1,500 Gateway instances.</cite> [3]

Anthropic Discloses Over 6,000 Vulnerabilities Found by AI

<cite index="31-4,31-5">As of October 2, 2026, Anthropic disclosed 6,157 vulnerabilities across 591 open source projects, with 516 of these patched.</cite> <cite index="31-6,31-7">Across all findings in the ledger, 584 identifiers have been issued: 219 CVE records and 365 GitHub Security Advisories, though in other cases maintainers shipped a fix without publishing an advisory.</cite> <cite index="34-1,34-3">Monthly CVE disclosures doubled in 2026, from 5,045 in January to 10,740 in August, and half of AI-discovered vulnerabilities lead to remote code execution.</cite> The disclosure comes amid growing evidence that AI-assisted vulnerability discovery is driving a surge in public CVE volumes across major software suppliers and open source projects. [4]

Splunk Patches Critical Unauthenticated RCE in Enterprise

<cite index="30-4">Splunk disclosed CVE-2026-76268, a missing authentication for critical function in the Patroni REST API in Splunk Enterprise, rated critical with a CVSS score of 9.8.</cite> The vulnerability affects Splunk Enterprise versions below 10.4.3, 10.2.7, and 10.0.10. Splunk's September/October 2026 security bulletin also addressed nine other vulnerabilities ranging from medium to high severity, including improper authorization, local privilege escalation, and authorization bypass issues across multiple Splunk Enterprise components and the Splunk Secure Gateway.
