Binance Breach Details Emerge, Pentagon Probes Base Freezer Hacks, DHS Leak Widens

North Korea link confirmed in record crypto theft, suspected attacks on military supply chains.

Binance discloses attack chain in $6.3 billion North Korean hack

Binance revealed that the largest cryptocurrency theft on record combined trojanized software, fake LinkedIn job interviews, and compromised internal AI tooling to breach DevOps engineers starting in June 2026. The FBI attributed the attack to TraderTraitor, the same North Korean group behind the $1.5 billion Bybit hack in 2025. Approximately $3 billion in stolen assets have been frozen across exchanges, but Binance has pledged to cover all losses. [1]

Pentagon treating military base freezer failures as suspected cyberattack

The Defense Department is investigating freezer malfunctions at at least six U.S. military commissaries in August as a possible hack, with a Cyber Command unit now working the case. The incidents spoiled food stores at bases including Fort Huachuca, Robins Air Force Base, and F.E. Warren Air Force Base. U.S. military investigators have contacted French counterparts after hackers pushed freezers at over 350 French supermarkets to 122 degrees in a confirmed attack, but no attribution has been made in the U.S. incidents. [2]

Hacktivists publish data on 41,000 DHS employees after breach

A group calling itself the Department of Peace released personnel records for over 41,000 Department of Homeland Security employees, including undercover investigators at ICE and CBP. The data was obtained through what hacktivists claim was a breach of an identity service used by DHS. The transparency nonprofit Distributed Denial of Secrets published a searchable database called Icepick on October 28, which was later taken offline after DHS filed a takedown request. [3]

Active exploitation of Node-RED flaw deploys Linux bots on industrial systems

Attackers are exploiting CVE-2026-58115, an authentication bypass in Node-RED programming software built into some industrial control products, to install backdoors and a Linux bot on unprotected servers. VulnCheck observed exploit traffic beginning October 30, with one attack address hitting eight different CVEs across industrial and web platforms. Siemens disclosed the critical flaw, warning it could allow unauthenticated remote code execution with maximum privileges. [4]

Android November updates patch critical Framework RCE and over 200 bugs

Google released November 2026 security updates addressing over 200 vulnerabilities across Android, including CVE-2026-58868, a critical remote code execution flaw in the Framework component requiring no user interaction. The patches arrive as the 2026-11-01 and 2026-11-05 security patch levels and cover defects in Android runtime, Kernel, and components from Arm, MediaTek, Qualcomm, and Unisoc. [5]
