McKesson confirms breach after ShinyHunters claims patient data theft

Healthcare distributor McKesson disclosed a cybersecurity incident on August 31 after detecting an intrusion on August 25. ShinyHunters claimed responsibility and alleged theft of 284 million patient records, though McKesson has not confirmed the scope or said the incident is material to operations. [1]
