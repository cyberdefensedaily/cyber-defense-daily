Intelligence agencies breached, Treasury hit, Bell confirms AI-assisted attack

String of government breaches and AI-powered intrusions mark busy 48 hours.

CIA, ODNI, NGA unclassified network breached

<cite index="9-1,9-4">The CIA, Office of the Director of National Intelligence, and National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency discovered a breach of an unclassified open-source intelligence analysis network in late November 2026.</cite> <cite index="9-2">Classified systems were not affected.</cite> <cite index="2-3">Investigators have not yet established who was responsible for the breach.</cite> The intrusion adds to a cluster of government cybersecurity incidents disclosed this week. [1]

Treasury's FinCEN investigating breach, systems offline

<cite index="40-4,40-5">The Financial Crimes Enforcement Network disclosed a cybersecurity incident on Tuesday that resulted in unauthorized access to a limited subset of its systems, causing FinCEN to take some systems offline as a precaution.</cite> <cite index="40-6">Treasury is working with the FBI, CISA, and third-party forensic examiners to determine the scope and impact.</cite> <cite index="40-8">A forensic analysis remains ongoing.</cite> <cite index="41-7">FinCEN has not disclosed whether any data was exfiltrated or linked the event to other reported breaches at intelligence agencies.</cite> [2]

Bell Canada confirms 'agentic' breach of circuit provisioning system

<cite index="46-1">Bell Canada confirmed that an "agentic" threat actor breached its systems on November 17, 2026, and stole enterprise customer data, including information from a system used to order and provision telecommunications circuits.</cite> <cite index="46-8">The company's description of the attacker as "agentic" suggests that AI-powered tools were used during the breach.</cite> <cite index="47-6">Bell stated that no credentials, sensitive personal information or file attachments were accessed during the incident.</cite> <cite index="52-8">Bell has not disclosed how many enterprise customers were impacted or the total amount of data stolen, and said it has not yet identified the attacker.</cite> [3]

CISA adds five newly exploited vulnerabilities to KEV

<cite index="57-1,57-2">CISA added three vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on November 24: an Idunno AntiCsrf deserialization flaw, a Drupal Core remote code execution vulnerability, and an Optimizely Web Experimentation critical state data issue.</cite> <cite index="56-2,56-3">On November 23, CISA added two more: a Project MONAI path traversal vulnerability and an N-able Mail Assure server-side request forgery flaw.</cite> Federal agencies face binding remediation deadlines for the listed flaws.
