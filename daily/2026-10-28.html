CISA Confirms Brotli Exploitation, DIA Flags China Space Lasers

Active exploitation of critical flaws and new intelligence on counterspace capabilities dominate the threat landscape.

CISA Adds Brotli and Adobe Commerce Flaws to Exploited List

CISA confirmed active exploitation of CVE-2026-111497, a critical Brotli decoder flaw that can corrupt memory and enable remote code execution, adding it to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on October 27. The agency also listed CVE-2026-48356, an Adobe Commerce and Magento file upload vulnerability dubbed PolyShell that attackers used to breach hundreds of online stores. Federal agencies must patch both by October 29. [1]

DIA: China Operates Ground-Based Anti-Satellite Lasers

The Defense Intelligence Agency's October 26 space threat assessment states China now operates multiple ground-based laser weapons capable of disrupting, degrading, or damaging satellites in low Earth orbit. The report projects China may field space-based directed-energy systems and higher-powered ground lasers threatening satellites in higher orbits by the late 2020s. The assessment covers China, Russia, Iran, and North Korea and is current through July 2026. [2]

Network Infrastructure Under Wave of Zero-Day Attacks

Multiple critical network infrastructure vendors confirmed active exploitation of zero-day flaws in October, including Ivanti Connect Secure VPNs, Arista VeloCloud Orchestrator, FortiMail, Cisco Secure Email Gateway, F5 BIG-IP, and Palo Alto Networks firewalls. BleepingComputer reports attackers are targeting these edge devices because they lack standard endpoint detection telemetry and provide access to internal networks. The PHP2Shell vulnerabilities disclosed October 21 put web management interfaces of many appliances at additional risk. [3]

Microsoft Patches Record 1,413 Flaws Including Exploited Zero-Day

Microsoft's October 2026 Patch Tuesday fixed 1,413 vulnerabilities, surpassing the company's previous annual record of 1,261 CVEs set in 2020. The release includes CVE-2026-96951, an actively exploited Windows Shell flaw allowing attackers to bypass Mark of the Web protections, plus two publicly disclosed zero-days in .NET Framework and MessagePack-CSharp. Security researchers note hundreds of CVEs per month has become the baseline rather than the exception. [4]
