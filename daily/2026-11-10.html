French Refrigeration Attack, Chrome Zero-Day, SonicWall MFA Bypass

France accuses Iran of infrastructure sabotage, Google patches eighth exploited Chrome flaw, and CISA flags VPN vulnerability.

France Formally Blames Iran for Refrigeration Cyberattack

<cite index="62-7,62-8">France's national cybersecurity agency attributed attacks on around 700 retail refrigeration systems to Shahid Kaveh, a cyber unit affiliated with Iran's Revolutionary Guard.</cite> <cite index="63-4,63-5">France's foreign minister called it "an attack of unprecedented scale and aggressiveness" and the first large-scale cyberattack aimed at sabotage on French soil.</cite> <cite index="59-3">Attackers remotely took over refrigeration controllers, turning up temperatures in the middle of the night and spoiling millions of dollars' worth of food.</cite> <cite index="61-4">The French government has summoned Iran's ambassador.</cite> [1]

Google Patches Eighth Exploited Chrome Zero-Day of 2026

<cite index="44-5,44-6">Google on Monday patched CVE-2026-125350, a high-severity use-after-free vulnerability in Chrome's XML component.</cite> <cite index="36-8">Google acknowledged an exploit exists in the wild but has not revealed specifics about how it's being abused or who is behind the attacks.</cite> <cite index="44-7">This marks the eighth Chrome zero-day exploited this year.</cite> [2]

CISA Flags Exploited SonicWall MFA Bypass Vulnerability

<cite index="48-3,48-4">CISA added CVE-2026-19868, a high-severity vulnerability impacting SonicWall SonicOS, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog following reports of possible exploitation.</cite> <cite index="48-5,48-6">The flaw allows a user with valid credentials for an alternate authentication method to bypass SAML authentication controls and SAML-enforced multi-factor authentication.</cite> <cite index="48-8">Federal agencies must apply patches by November 20, 2026.</cite> [3]

SAP Patches Four Maximum-Severity Vulnerabilities

<cite index="29-1,29-2">SAP's November 2026 Security Patch Day released on November 10 includes 35 new security notes, with four vulnerabilities rated CVSS 10.0.</cite> <cite index="29-4,29-5">Among the most serious issues is CVE-2026-76964, a memory corruption vulnerability in SAP NetWeaver AS ABAP's Internet Communication Manager, and CVE-2026-89126, affecting SAP Web Dispatcher.</cite> <cite index="29-3">SAP urges customers to review its security support portal and apply relevant corrections promptly.</cite> [4]

N-able Takes Servers Offline After 30+ Zero-Days Published

<cite index="31-1,31-2">N-able took its hosted N-central environments offline and urged on-premises customers to shut down servers after a security researcher publicly disclosed more than 30 alleged zero-day vulnerabilities, including authentication bypasses, credential disclosure, and remote code execution flaws.</cite> <cite index="31-3">One critical exploit chain abuses an unauthenticated XXE vulnerability in N-central's service-import functionality to steal a trusted local session, reset an account password, and execute commands on the server.</cite> <cite index="31-5">The researcher claims he disclosed two RCE vulnerabilities and a privilege-escalation flaw five to six months ago that remain unpatched.</cite> [5]
