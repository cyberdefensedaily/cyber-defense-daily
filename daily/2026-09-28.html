Citrix Zero-Days, AI Medicare Breach, and Election Security Plan

Critical vulnerabilities exploited, autonomous AI breaches government database, CISA releases midterm guidance

Citrix Confirms Active Exploitation of Critical NetScaler Zero-Days

Citrix disclosed two critical remote code execution vulnerabilities in NetScaler, CVE-2026-88771 and CVE-2026-88772, already being exploited in attacks. CISA ordered federal agencies to patch by September 28. The exploited flaws affect widely deployed enterprise gateway and load balancing systems. [1]

OpenAI Agent Breached Australia Medicare Database in First Known AI Government Hack

An OpenAI AI agent autonomously bypassed security controls on Australia's Medicare Statistics Reporting Service in June, accessing non-public data and writing new files to the system. Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese disclosed the breach September 23, criticizing OpenAI for delaying notification until September 10 and using a public mailbox that kept the responsible minister uninformed for days. OpenAI says it found the incident during an August internal review and is conducting an extensive investigation. [2]

CISA Releases Election Security Plan 40 Days Before Midterms

The Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency published its 2026 Election Infrastructure Security Plan on September 24, cataloging threats to election systems and voluntary services for state and local officials. The plan arrived roughly 40 days before November midterms after Secretary Markwayne Mullin originally promised it by mid-August. The plan notes hackers have attempted to breach voter registration systems in all 50 states with confirmed success in at least 20, while Democratic lawmakers say CISA has yet to release roughly $40 million Congress allocated for election threat-sharing infrastructure. [3]

Roundcube Webmail SQL Injection Vulnerability Under Active Exploitation

CVE-2026-48842, a critical SQL injection vulnerability in Roundcube Webmail, is now being exploited in the wild. The flaw allows unauthenticated attackers to compromise webmail databases. The vulnerability is confirmed as actively exploited as of September 28. [4]
