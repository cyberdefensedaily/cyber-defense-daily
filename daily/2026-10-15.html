Palo Alto zero-day exploited, North Korean laptop farms raided, GitHub supply chain attack escalates

Active exploitation hits critical infrastructure as law enforcement disrupts state-backed fraud schemes

Palo Alto Networks patches actively exploited PAN-OS firewall vulnerability

Palo Alto Networks disclosed CVE-2026-0322, a vulnerability in PAN-OS management interfaces with a CVSS score of 8.6, confirmed under active exploitation. The flaw allows authenticated attackers with network access to execute arbitrary code with root privileges on PA-Series and VM-Series firewalls and Panorama appliances. CISA added the vulnerability to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on October 14 with a federal remediation deadline of October 17, 2026. [1]

FBI raids 40 North Korean IT worker laptop farms, charges 14 in Texas operation

The Department of Justice charged 14 individuals and seized 187 laptops in raids across 40 Texas locations between September 21 and 25, disrupting North Korean remote IT worker schemes. Two US nationals, Michael James Bostelle and Juan Cecilio Pena Carrillo, were arrested for allegedly hosting employer-issued laptops to help North Korean workers appear US-based. The multi-year scheme affected more than 70 US companies and generated over $3 million in fraudulent revenue, according to the indictment. [2]

GhostAction supply chain attack compromises 1,900 GitHub accounts, targets 30,000 repositories

The GhostAction credential-theft campaign spiked on October 8 and 9, compromising approximately 1,900 GitHub user accounts to inject malicious workflows into over 30,000 repositories across 3,000 users and organizations. The campaign, first disclosed in September 2025, uses stolen personal access tokens to push fake security audit workflows that exfiltrate CI/CD secrets, cloud credentials, and API keys. GitGuardian reports the attackers attempted to extract 3,174 secrets from public repositories' continuous integration systems. [3]

Microsoft patches record 1,413 vulnerabilities including exploited zero-day

Microsoft's October 2026 Patch Tuesday addressed 1,413 CVEs, including CVE-2026-96951, a Windows Shell command injection vulnerability actively exploited to bypass Mark of the Web protections. CISA added the zero-day to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog with a federal remediation deadline of October 16. The update also included 75 Critical vulnerabilities and two additional publicly disclosed zero-days affecting .NET Framework and MessagePack-CSharp. [4]

Pentagon seeks $50 billion for defense autonomy, reorganizes command structure

Secretary of War Pete Hegseth told the Association of the U.S. Army annual meeting on October 14 that the department is requesting roughly $50 billion in reconciliation funding to scale autonomous and robotic systems. The Defense Autonomous Warfare Group, previously run by the Defense Innovation Unit, is being absorbed into a new Task Force Autonomous Warfare inside Special Operations Command ahead of establishing the Autonomous Warfare Command. The Army separately announced it is establishing Futures and Autonomous Systems Command and designated Fort Rucker as its UAS Autonomy Center of Excellence. [5]
