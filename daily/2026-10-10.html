FBI Disrupts China-Linked Hacking Tools, GitHub Attack Spreads

Federal action against state-sponsored infrastructure and fresh supply chain attacks dominate.

FBI Seizes Seven Domains Used by China-Linked Flax Typhoon

The FBI and DOJ seized seven domains tied to MicroScan and FishHub hacking platforms allegedly operated by China-based Integrity Technology Group. The tools were used to scan critical infrastructure including a South Carolina power utility, airports, and universities. CISA added five vulnerabilities exploited by Flax Typhoon to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, giving federal agencies until October 11 to patch or discontinue use. [1]

Seven Nations Warn of China-Linked Email Theft Campaign

The FBI, CISA, NSA, and agencies from six other countries issued a joint advisory October 8 linking a multi-year email theft campaign to Integrity Technology Group. Hackers stole email from government, law enforcement, healthcare, and religious organizations in Southeast Asia. The advisory documents custom tools including a scanner with over 1,300 scripts and a web portal that gave third parties access to stolen inboxes. [2]

GhostAction Supply Chain Attack Hits Tens of Thousands of GitHub Repositories

Attackers compromised over 500 GitHub accounts and pushed malicious workflows to tens of thousands of repositories since October 7, according to Socket. The campaign targets CI/CD secrets, cloud credentials, and AI API keys. An October 8 burst compromised two high-profile maintainer accounts including the author of 18,000-star project pyxel. The new variant mines credentials from entire Git histories, including secrets deleted years ago. [3]

Hackers Exploit Unpatched AhsayCBS Backup Flaws for Remote Code Execution

Threat actors are exploiting two unpatched vulnerabilities in AhsayCBS backup software to bypass authentication and execute arbitrary code with System privileges. CVE-2026-105133 and CVE-2026-105134 affect all versions through 10.3.4. Huntress observed exploitation starting October 7, with attackers deploying web shells and XMRig cryptocurrency miners on at least five organizations as of October 8. [4]

Android October Updates Patch Critical Privilege Escalation Flaw

Google released Android security updates resolving 25 vulnerabilities including a critical-severity privilege escalation bug in the System component. The flaw could lead to local escalation of privilege with no additional execution privileges and no user interaction needed for exploitation. The 2026-10-01 patch level also addresses six critical-severity bugs in System and one in Framework. [5]
