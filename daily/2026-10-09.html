FBI seizes Chinese hacking tools, Oracle breach hits 20M, Pentagon launches cyber bonus program

Justice Department disrupts state-sponsored infrastructure targeting US critical systems

FBI disrupts Chinese hacking tools used against critical infrastructure

The Justice Department and FBI seized seven domains supporting MicroScan and FishHub, hacking tools operated by China-based Integrity Technology Group and used by Flax Typhoon actors to scan and breach US and foreign critical infrastructure. The court-authorized action announced October 8 targeted infrastructure used to identify vulnerabilities in power companies, airports, and universities across the US, Taiwan, Japan and Poland. Integrity Tech has contracts with the Chinese government and built a botnet of IoT devices infected with Mirai malware to facilitate the scanning operations. [1]

Oracle Health breach exposes medical data of nearly 20 million

A cyberattack on Oracle Health's legacy Cerner systems compromised personal and medical information of nearly 20 million people. The breach affected Oracle's healthcare infrastructure and exposed sensitive patient data. The company has not disclosed when the attack occurred or how long unauthorized access persisted. [2]

Pentagon launches cash bonus program for innovative cyber warriors

The Department of Defense approved a new Cyber Operations-Peculiar Awards program effective October 5 that allows cash bonuses up to $2,500 for service members who make exceptional contributions to cyberspace operations. The directive approved September 30 covers novel actions, inventions, or technical achievements that enhance offensive cyber operations, defend networks, or neutralize previously unknown advanced persistent threats. All cyberspace operations forces are eligible regardless of rank. [3]

Android October patches fix critical privilege escalation flaw

Google's October security updates resolve 25 vulnerabilities in Android Framework and System components, including seven critical-severity flaws. The most severe is a critical bug in the System component that could enable local privilege escalation with no additional execution privileges needed. Pixel devices received fixes for six additional vulnerabilities including three critical issues affecting Bluetooth, GDMC, and GSA components. [4]

Cisco urges patching of critical NetScaler vulnerability

Citrix is urging immediate patching of CVE-2026-107406, a critical vulnerability in NetScaler ADC and NetScaler Gateway that could lead to remote code execution or denial-of-service. The security defect requires urgent attention from organizations running affected systems. Cisco separately patched a dozen critical vulnerabilities across its product line that could enable unauthorized access, privilege escalation, and remote code execution. [5]
