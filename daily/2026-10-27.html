Critical vulnerabilities under active exploitation as military cyber operations expand

Critical PHP vulnerabilities exploited in the wild

PHP released emergency updates October 21 for CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, both rated 9.2 CVSS, allowing unauthenticated remote code execution on web servers. The flaws affect all PHP versions from 5.4 through 8.5.11, impacting an estimated 70% of websites. CISA added both to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog October 26 after confirming active exploitation, ordering federal agencies to patch by October 29. [1]

US Army developing autonomous AI cyber offensive tools

Army Cyber Command is developing ECHOTRIBBLE, AI agents designed to autonomously infiltrate enemy networks, identify targets, and neutralize them with minimal human oversight. Documents describe agents capable of moving between devices and operating in cloud systems and air-gapped networks. The project involves Army Cyber Command, the Army Cyber Technology and Innovation Center, and the Cyber Fusion Innovation Center. [2]

CISA releases new strategy emphasizing proactive defense

CISA released its CISA 2030 strategy October 26, promising to shift from passive defense toward identifying and removing adversaries from critical infrastructure networks before disruptions occur. The plan emphasizes prioritizing infrastructure whose disruption would most threaten national security and economy. CISA will review its core services within 60 days and complete an implementation plan with metrics within 180 days. [3]

Leidos wins $160M Cyber Command contract

US Cyber Command awarded Leidos a five-year, $160 million contract October 21 to support full-spectrum offensive and defensive cyber operations. The work includes operational planning, cyber defense, threat analysis, and securing the Department of War Information Network. Leidos will incorporate recently acquired Kudu Dynamics capabilities into the effort. [4]
