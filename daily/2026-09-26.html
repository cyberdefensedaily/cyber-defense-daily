North Korean Breach, Critical Flaws Under Exploit

Bitget loses $387M, CISA flags enterprise vulnerabilities already exploited in the wild

North Korea Linked to $387M Bitget Cryptocurrency Theft

Cryptocurrency exchange Bitget confirmed attackers stole approximately $387.5 million from hot and warm wallets on September 24, with CEO Gracy Chen attributing the breach to suspected North Korean threat actors based on IP patterns and on-chain analysis. The attackers compromised a backend system and manipulated transaction data to trigger fraudulent approvals rather than stealing private keys directly. Bitget said its User Protection Fund covers the loss and that cold wallets holding the majority of customer assets were not affected. [1]

CISA Adds Exploited WSO2 and Adobe Commerce Flaws to KEV Catalog

The Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency added two critical vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on September 24 based on evidence of active exploitation. CVE-2026-5430, a path traversal flaw in WSO2 products with a CVSS score of 9.8, allows unrestricted file upload and remote code execution. CVE-2026-71362, an incorrect authorization vulnerability in Adobe Commerce and Magento with a CVSS score of 9.1, permits elevated access without user interaction. Federal agencies must remediate by September 27. [2]

Microsoft September Patch Tuesday Addresses 972 Vulnerabilities

Microsoft released security updates for 972 vulnerabilities in its September 2026 Patch Tuesday, including two actively exploited zero-days and 113 critical flaws. The release includes 21 critical remote code execution vulnerabilities spanning Outlook, Word, Excel, and Windows components, with 12 of those triggerable through Preview Pane or Reading Pane without user interaction. CVSS scores for the critical vulnerabilities range from 6.5 to 9.8. [3]
